Nau mai, haere mai — welcome to Te Puia, the home of Ngā Whakairoiro o Aotearoa, where the stories, skills, and spirit of Māori culture are kept alive and shared with the world. Nestled in the geothermal valley of Te Whakarewarewa, Te Puia is a place where nature and tradition stand side by side: the powerful breath of Pōhutu Geyser, the artistry of master carvers and weavers, and the warmth of manaakitanga that has guided our people for generations.
